How MAHA Works

  1. Government creates and publishes challenge
  2. AI-assisted discovery identifies potentially relevant startups with evidence
  3. Officials verify eligibility and evaluator assessments
  4. Pilot milestones and KPI evidence are tracked
  5. Procurement handoff package is prepared for authorised decision
